Frequently Asked Questions

How do I scan a document?

Tap the Scan button at the bottom of the screen. Point your camera at the document — ScanSmith will automatically detect the edges and capture when the document is steady. You can also tap the shutter button manually.

Can I scan multiple pages?

Yes. After scanning one page, keep scanning more pages in the same session. When you're done, tap Save. ScanSmith creates a single multi-page PDF automatically.

How does OCR work?

Tap "OCR" (or "Extract Text") on any document. ScanSmith uses Apple's Vision framework to recognize text in your scans — including handwriting. Everything is processed on your device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Where are my documents stored?

On your device only, inside the app's private storage. We never upload, sync, or access your files. Your documents are as private as anything on your phone.

Is there a subscription?

No. ScanSmith is a one-time $4.99 purchase. You pay once, you own it forever. No subscriptions, no monthly fees, no annual renewals. Every feature is included.

How do I export or share?

Tap the Share button on any document. You can send via email, AirDrop, Messages, save to Files, print, or share to any app installed on your device. Export as PDF, JPEG, PNG, or plain text.

Can I sign documents?

Yes. Tap "Sign" on any document to create your signature with your finger or Apple Pencil. Save multiple signatures for reuse. Place them anywhere on any PDF page.

Do you support Apple Pencil?

Yes. Apple Pencil is fully supported for signatures and freehand annotations on iPad.

I need a refund.

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request a refund for any App Store purchase.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Simply download ScanSmith on your new device using the same Apple ID. Your purchase will be restored automatically. You can also go to Settings and your purchase will sync via your Apple ID.

Is my data safe?

Yes. ScanSmith makes zero network connections (except Apple's StoreKit for purchase verification, which Apple handles). Your documents never leave your device. We can't see them even if we wanted to.
