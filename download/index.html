Downloads

Software from the builder behind DialectForge. I built these apps myself. They're how I fund the work on DialectForge. The iOS apps here are built to keep your files on your device, with no cloud uploads, analytics or tracking. Each app's privacy policy has the details.

DialectForge OS (DFoS)

x86_64 · v1.0 Troposphere · ~460 MB compressed · 8 GB flashed

Custom Linux kernel with dialect-encrypted memory, a native encrypted filesystem and a three-tier trust model. Experimental and not independently tested. Flash to USB and boot. About DFoS

VPN

DialectForge VPN Offline

macOS

Censorship-resistant VPN designed to make your traffic hard to identify. Its seed-negotiated dialect changes every 2 minutes, so there's no fixed pattern for censors to fingerprint. DialectForge LLC doesn't run any VPN servers anymore. The server and client source will be published under AGPL-3.0 as dialectforge/dialectforge-vpn-oss so you can run your own.
