Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if I forget my PIN?

Your PIN is stored as a cryptographic hash and cannot be recovered. If you forget your PIN, your encrypted data cannot be accessed. This is by design to protect your privacy.

What is the decoy vault?

SnapGuard has two vaults. Your real PIN opens your real vault. Any other PIN opens a decoy vault with separate content. If someone forces you to open the app, they see the decoy, and the app is designed not to reveal that a real vault exists.

Does SnapGuard collect any data?

No. Zero data collection, zero analytics, zero tracking. Everything stays on your device. We have no servers and no access to your content.

How does the encryption work?

SnapGuard uses dual-layer encryption. The first layer uses a proprietary key-derivation system, and the second layer applies AES-256-GCM encryption. Your PIN never leaves the device and is stored as a SHA-256 hash in the iOS Keychain.

How do I use the Share Extension?

From any app, tap the Share button and select SnapGuard. Enter your PIN to save the file directly to your vault. Your real PIN saves to the real vault; any other PIN saves to the decoy vault.

How does WiFi Transfer work?

Open WiFi Transfer from the menu inside your vault. It starts a local server on your phone. Open the displayed URL on your computer's browser (both devices must be on the same WiFi network). Your files are decrypted on-the-fly for download and never leave your local network.

Is the private browser really private?

Yes. The browser uses an ephemeral session with no persistent cookies, no cache, and no history. When you close it, everything is gone. You can save page screenshots or files directly to your vault.

What does Shake to Wipe do?

When enabled, shaking your device triggers a panic wipe that can clear your vault contents. This is a premium feature designed for emergency situations.

Troubleshooting

SnapGuard doesn't appear in the Share Sheet

Open Settings > General > SnapGuard and make sure it's enabled. If it still doesn't appear, try restarting your device. The Share Extension supports photos, videos, and files.

WiFi Transfer isn't connecting

Make sure both your phone and computer are on the same WiFi network. Check that no VPN is active. Try the URL exactly as shown in the app, including the port number.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone > tap your name at the top > Subscriptions > SnapGuard > Cancel Subscription. You'll keep access until the end of your current billing period.

How do I restore my subscription on a new device?

Open SnapGuard > Settings > tap "Restore Purchase." Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used for the original purchase.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled directly by Apple — visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request one.
