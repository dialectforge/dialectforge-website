Account & Billing

How do I reset my password?

On the login screen, tap "Forgot Password?" and enter your email. You'll receive a reset link within a few minutes.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to your iPhone Settings → Your Name → Subscriptions → DialectForge VPN → Cancel Subscription. You'll keep access until the end of your billing period.

How do I restore my purchase?

In the app, go to Settings and tap "Restore Purchases". This will restore your subscription if you've reinstalled the app or switched devices.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request a refund.
