Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Go to Settings in the game and tap "Restore Purchase." This will check your Apple ID for previous purchases and unlock the full game if you've already bought it.

How do I earn more boosters?

Boosters are earned by completing levels with high star ratings. Getting 3 stars on a level awards bonus boosters. There is no way to buy boosters — they're earned through gameplay only.

How does the Workshop work?

Stars earned from completing levels can be spent to build and upgrade items in your Workshop. Each item has 3 upgrade levels. The Workshop is your personal space to customize as you progress through the game.

What are Daily Challenges?

Each day, a new puzzle challenge is generated. Complete it to build your streak. Difficulty varies by day of the week — weekdays are easier, weekends are harder. Building streaks earns achievements.

My progress was lost. Can you recover it?

Game progress is stored locally on your device. If you delete the app, progress is lost and cannot be recovered by us. We recommend keeping regular iCloud backups of your device.

Does Workshop Blast have ads?

No. Workshop Blast has zero ads and will never have ads. The game is supported by the one-time purchase price.

Does the game collect my data?

No. Workshop Blast collects zero personal data. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled directly by Apple — visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request one.
