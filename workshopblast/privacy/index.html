WORKSHOP BLAST

Last updated: February 2026

Workshop Blast does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. We don't track you. We don't have analytics. We don't have servers. Your game data stays on your device.

Workshop Blast ("the App") is developed by John Dean Martin ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the App.

Data Collection

Workshop Blast collects zero personal data. We do not use analytics frameworks, tracking pixels, advertising SDKs, or any form of data collection. There are no accounts to create and no information to provide.

Data Storage

All game progress (level completion, stars, workshop upgrades, achievements, and daily challenge streaks) is stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage mechanisms. This data never leaves your device.

Third-Party Services

Workshop Blast uses Apple's StoreKit framework solely for processing the one-time game purchase. Apple handles this transaction according to their own privacy policy. We do not receive or store any payment information.

Children's Privacy

Workshop Blast is safe for all ages. Since we collect no data whatsoever, there are no special considerations needed for children's privacy. The game contains no user-generated content, social features, or external links beyond this privacy policy and support page.

Advertising

Workshop Blast contains zero advertisements. We will never add ads to the game.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. Given our commitment to zero data collection, significant changes are unlikely.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@dialectforge.com.
