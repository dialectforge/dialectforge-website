PIXELSMITH

Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 9, 2026

PixelSmith processes all images entirely on your device. We collect zero data.

PixelSmith ("the App") is developed by John Dean Martin ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the App.

Information We Collect

PixelSmith is designed with privacy as a core principle. The App processes all images entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your images, photos, or personal data on external servers.

Camera and Photo Library Access

The App may request access to your device's camera and photo library solely for the purpose of selecting or capturing images for editing within the App. These images never leave your device unless you explicitly choose to share or export them.

Purchase Information

PixelSmith offers an optional in-app purchase for Pro access. All transactions are processed securely by Apple through the App Store. We do not have access to your payment details, credit card information, or Apple ID.

Analytics and Tracking

PixelSmith does not include any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking frameworks. We do not track your usage patterns, collect device identifiers, or build user profiles.

Data Storage

All app data, including your preferences and trial status, is stored locally on your device. No data is synced to external servers or cloud services.

Children's Privacy

PixelSmith does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. The App does not require account creation or collect any personal data from any user.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@dialectforge.com.
