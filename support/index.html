Frequently Asked Questions

I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

On the login screen, tap "Forgot Password?" and enter your email. You'll receive a reset link within a few minutes. Check your spam folder if you don't see it.

How do I cancel my subscription?

iOS: Go to Settings → Your Name → Subscriptions → DialectForge VPN → Cancel
macOS (App Store): Open App Store → Account → Subscriptions
Other: Contact support and we'll help you

Does DialectForge log my activity?

No. We do not log your browsing activity, DNS queries, or traffic content. We only store the minimum data needed to operate your account (email, device IDs, connection timestamps). See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases (iOS) are handled directly by Apple — visit reportaproblem.apple.com. macOS purchases through GitHub releases are non-refundable.
