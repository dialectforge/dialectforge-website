Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a subscription?

No. SoundBits is a one-time purchase of $3.99. All features are included with no recurring charges, no trials, and no in-app purchases. You pay once and own it forever.

Does SoundBits collect any data?

No. Zero data collection, zero analytics, zero tracking. Everything stays on your device. There are no servers, no cloud sync, and no network requests of any kind.

How does transcription work?

SoundBits uses Apple's built-in speech recognition with on-device processing. Your audio is never sent to any server. Transcription quality depends on audio clarity, background noise, and language. It works entirely offline.

What audio formats does SoundBits support?

Recordings are saved in M4A (AAC) format by default. You can export recordings as M4A or convert to WAV. Transcriptions can be exported as plain text.

Can I record in the background?

Yes. SoundBits supports background recording. Once you start a recording, you can switch to other apps or lock your device and the recording continues.

How do I trim a recording?

Open a recording, tap the trim (scissors) button, and adjust the start and end sliders. Tap "Save Trim" to apply. The trimmed file replaces the original, so export a copy first if you want to keep the full recording.

What happens if I delete the app?

All recordings and transcriptions are stored locally on your device. If you delete the app, your data will be permanently lost. We recommend exporting important recordings before uninstalling.

What audio quality options are available?

SoundBits offers three quality levels: Standard (suitable for voice memos), High (better clarity), and Lossless (maximum quality, larger file size). You can set your preferred quality in Settings.

Troubleshooting

Transcription isn't working

Make sure you've granted SoundBits permission for Speech Recognition in Settings > SoundBits > Speech Recognition. On-device speech recognition also requires that the relevant language model is downloaded on your device (Settings > General > Keyboard > Dictation).

Recording isn't starting

Make sure you've granted SoundBits microphone access in Settings > SoundBits > Microphone. If the issue persists, try closing and reopening the app.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Since SoundBits is a paid app (not a free download with in-app purchases), simply re-download it from the App Store using the same Apple ID. You won't be charged again — the App Store recognizes your previous purchase.
