Frequently Asked Questions

Does VoxBitz collect any data?

No. Zero data collection, zero analytics, zero tracking. Everything stays on your device. We have no servers and no access to your recordings.

How does transcription work?

VoxBitz uses Apple's on-device speech recognition. Your audio is processed entirely on your device and never sent to any server. Transcriptions appear as synced lyrics that scroll along with playback.

Can I record with AirPods or Bluetooth headphones?

Yes. VoxBitz automatically detects Bluetooth audio inputs including AirPods, AirPods Pro, and other Bluetooth microphones.

How does the Apple Watch app work?

Open VoxBitz on your Apple Watch, tap to record, and tap again to stop. The recording automatically syncs to your iPhone over WatchConnectivity.

How do I use the widget?

Long-press your home screen, tap the + button, search for VoxBitz, and add the widget. Tapping it opens the app and starts recording immediately.

How does batch export work?

In the Library tab, select multiple recordings and tap the share button to export them all at once.

What happens to my recordings if I delete the app?

All recordings are stored locally on your device. If you delete VoxBitz, your recordings will be permanently lost. We recommend exporting important recordings regularly.

Is there a subscription?

No. VoxBitz is a one-time purchase of $3.99 with no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, and no recurring fees. Every feature is included.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled directly by Apple — visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request one.
