VOXBITZ

Last updated: February 19, 2026

VoxBitz processes everything on your device. We collect zero data, use zero analytics, and have zero access to your recordings or transcriptions.

VoxBitz ("the App") is developed by John Dean Martin ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the App.

Information We Collect

VoxBitz is designed with privacy as its core principle. All audio recordings, transcriptions, and metadata are stored entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your recordings, transcriptions, or personal data on external servers. We collect no data whatsoever.

Microphone Access

The App requests access to your device's microphone solely to record audio. Audio is captured, processed, and stored entirely on your device. Recordings are never transmitted to any server or third party.

Speech Recognition

VoxBitz uses Apple's on-device speech recognition to transcribe your recordings. Transcription happens entirely on your device using Apple's built-in speech framework with on-device processing enabled. No audio data is sent to Apple's servers or any other external service for transcription.

Background Audio

The App may use background audio capabilities to continue recording when the app is not in the foreground. This processing happens entirely on your device.

Purchase Information

VoxBitz is a one-time paid purchase ($3.99) with no subscriptions, in-app purchases, or trials. The purchase transaction is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not have access to your payment details, credit card information, or Apple ID.

Analytics and Tracking

VoxBitz does not include any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking frameworks. We do not track your usage patterns, collect device identifiers, or build user profiles. There are no analytics of any kind.

Data Storage

All app data, including your audio recordings, transcriptions, folder organization, and preferences, is stored locally on your device. No data is synced to external servers or cloud services.

Network Access

VoxBitz does not make any network requests. The App functions entirely offline. There is no server infrastructure, no API calls, and no internet connection required to use any feature.

Third-Party Services

VoxBitz does not integrate with any third-party services, SDKs, or analytics platforms. The only external dependency is Apple's on-device speech recognition framework, which processes data locally on your device.

Children's Privacy

VoxBitz does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. The App does not require account creation or collect any personal data from any user.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@dialectforge.com.
